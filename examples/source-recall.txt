Learning from evidence

An observation describes something that can be noticed or measured. A measurement records a quantity using a chosen unit. An explanation proposes why an observation occurred.

A fair comparison changes one factor while keeping other important conditions alike. Repeating a comparison helps reveal variation between trials. Keeping a complete record helps others understand how a conclusion was reached.

Source recall is different from checking whether every statement in a source is true. Compare an explanation with the original evidence, and write down questions when a connection is unclear.
